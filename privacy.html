Privacy Policy

Auto Shorts (shown on Google's consent screen as “youtube upload”) · Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What this app is

Auto Shorts is a desktop application used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channel “Sage Is Good”, to prepare and publish short videos from their own live streams. It runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. What information the app accesses

When the owner connects their account, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, and
  • read information about the owner's own channel and videos (for example the list of live streams, the schedule of videos already uploaded, and view and like counts).

The app also publishes the same videos to the owner's own Instagram account and Facebook Page using Meta's official tools, and reads view and follower counts for those accounts.

The app does not access any other person's account or information.

3. How the information is used

The information is used only to do what the owner asks: find new live streams, upload the finished videos with their titles and descriptions, schedule them, and show the owner how their videos are performing. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Where the information is stored

Everything, including the sign-in permission (access token) the app receives from Google and Meta, is stored only on the owner's computer. The developer does not run any server that receives, stores or processes this information.

5. Sharing

The information is not sold, rented, shared or transferred to anyone. It is only sent to Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram and Facebook) and, for writing titles, to an AI model that runs on the same computer, as required to publish the owner's own videos.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Auto Shorts' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Keeping and deleting information

The owner can disconnect the app at any time by removing its access in their Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and deleting the app's files from their computer. Doing so removes all information the app holds.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
