Auto Shorts

A personal tool for the gaming channel “Sage Is Good”.

Auto Shorts is a small desktop application that runs on the channel owner's own computer. After a live stream ends, it cuts the best moments into short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts and Reels), writes a title and description for each one, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page on a schedule.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no website to log in to, and no other users. The app asks Google for permission to upload videos to, and read basic information about, the owner's own YouTube channel.

Your data

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server run by the developer. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
